# Car sprite source

- Asset name: `car_default.png` (seven near-rear turning views, 1050 × 107 px).
- Author: Drummyfish.
- Source: OpenGameArt **2.5D Racing Resources**.
- Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/25d-racing-resources
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/racing%20resources_0.zip
- Retrieved: 2026-09-23.
- SHA-256: `e399b8155b2b30d38c6125b192294bf523f6a90d4117ddb365c219c7944b9644`.

The PNG is imported byte-for-byte, without repainting, rescaling, mirroring, or
recoloring. Each frame occupies a 150 × 107 cell. Frame selection uses texture UVs.
Only the car PNG is included; no scenery, roads, UI, shadows, or other pack assets
are imported. The archive directory and car entry were retrieved with HTTP byte
ranges, without downloading/extracting the complete resource pack.

The sheet provides seven small turns around a rear view. It is NOT an eight-way
or 360-degree sheet. Opponent direction bins explicitly reuse its nearest rear
quarter views; true side/front art is still needed for a faithful passing view.
